# Stage76 — F&O portfolio risk and virtual-capital research fence (NO-GO)

Date 2026-11-04. Stacked on Stage75 draft PR #67; not merged to \`main\`.

## Implemented
- \`src/nexus_titan/operations/stage76_fo_portfolio_risk_fence.py\` consumes **operator-supplied Stage75 offline readiness reports** for up to eight independent NIFTY/BANKNIFTY long CE/PE ideas (policy configurable within bounds). This is a pure read-only screen, **not** a portfolio ledger or broker/risk enforcement process.
- Enforces identical account/virtual-capital assumption across packets; rejects duplicate underlying/strike/side, mismatched Stage75 capital allocation arithmetic, malformed SHA-256 references, missing Stage75 mandatory global risk/atomic-capital blockers, forged trading approval flags, and reports with nonzero orders or purported real fills.
- Aggregates synthetic long-premium debits and **sum of each idea's individually worst stress loss** in integer paise with conservative upward-rounded basis points. Enforces a separate per-idea, per-underlying, total-debit and total-stressed-loss policy. Maximum theoretical **long-premium** loss is recorded as total debit. This does not model margin, open positions, futures, short-option unlimited losses or calibrated joint portfolio VaR.
- Applies an explicitly **untrusted** caller-supplied daily loss, drawdown, consecutive-loss, kill switch and risk-engine health screen. No telemetry is independently authenticated. Even local pass never authorizes paper/live orders.
- Includes adversarial synthetic NIFTY/BANKNIFTY tests for duplicate ideas, overspending across two individually passing reports, market concentration, stress losses, kill switch, degraded risk engine, malformed risk policy, changed approvals and cross-account capital; Python 3.11/3.12/3.13 CI no-data smoke.

## Important limits
- Stage75 reports can be freely created in Python. Their digest and Stage71–74 hash labels are **not signed source attestations**. The Stage76 calculation cannot prove they were generated by the intended code or that market quotes/labels are genuine.
- **No global F&O Risk & Veto has been implemented or verified.** Legacy \`VirtualAccount.submit_order/approve_order/execute_fill\`, optional \`PaperIntentGateway\`, crypto, research books, and independent Stage53/Stage55 synthetic ledgers remain separate code paths. Stage76 is NOT wired into their actual order/fill entrypoints and does not block them. Global non-bypassability and single atomic capital authority are still P0.
- Aggregated hypothetical premium debit is only a **what-if allocation**. It does not deduct reserved capital, account for all pending orders, current positions, financing, fees beyond Stage75 illustrative inputs, exercise/settlement, or reconcile an actual broker. There is no real shadow P&L or strategy learning.
- An individually maximum-loss-per-scenario sum is conservative relative to the selected finite synthetic cases, but cannot establish an actual worst market outcome or a valid risk quantile. Risk/market data are caller-declared.
- Stage76 provides **NO-GO only** for strategy promotion, paper/live trading, release/merge authorization and money movement regardless of green tests.
